### IEBC 7-Step Voting Guide

1. Entry and Identification
- Enter the polling station and present your original National ID card or a valid Kenyan Passport (the document you used during registration).
- Polling stations typically open at 6:00 AM and close at 5:00 PM.

2. Verification (KIEMS Kit)
- A polling clerk will use the KIEMS (Kenya Integrated Election Management System) kit to verify your details biometrically (fingerprints).
- If biometrics fail, the clerk will use the alphanumeric search (ID number) as a secondary verification.

3. Issuance of Ballot Papers
- Once verified, you will be issued six (6) stamped ballot papers, each color-coded for a specific elective position:
    - White: President
    - Blue: Governor
    - Yellow: Senator
    - Pink: Member of National Assembly (MP)
    - Green: County Woman Representative
    - Beige: Member of County Assembly (MCA)

4. Marking the Ballot
- Proceed to a private voting booth.
- Mark your preferred candidate with a tick (✓) or a cross (✗).
- Important: Use only one symbol per paper and stay inside the box. Marking outside or using multiple symbols may spoil your vote.

5. Casting the Vote
- Fold your ballot papers and drop them into the corresponding color-coded ballot boxes.
- Ensure the color of the ballot paper matches the color of the box lid to avoid your vote being declared "stray" or spoilt.

6. Indelible Ink
- A clerk will mark your small finger with indelible ink to show that you have already voted.

7. Exit
- Leave the polling station immediately after voting to allow others to cast their ballots.

### Quick Eligibility Checklist
- Age: Must be 18 years or older.
- Registration: Must be a registered voter in the specific polling station.
- Documents: Must have an original ID or Passport (photocopies are not accepted).
- Status: You can check your registration status by texting your ID/Passport Number to the official IEBC SMS code (usually 70000) or visiting the IEBC online portal.
